## Hercules DJ Control Pro: Compatible with Djay, Serato & Mixxx – Advanced PCDJ-Supported Controller

[![Hercules DJControl JogVision with DEX 3](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/djcontroljogvisionsupported-template.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/djcontroljogvisionsupported-template.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "Hercules DJControl JogVision with DEX 3")

**The DJControl JogVision from Hercules is their most advanced DJ controller to date, and it’s now zero-configure with DEX 3.7.0.1 (and up)**

While Hercules has primarily been known for entry-level or beginner [DJ controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), the **DJControl JogVision** is one serious bit of kit at an affordable price point (most sites list it online at only $299 USD).

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Hercules DJControl JogVision Top](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/preview.jpg?fit=300%2C300&ssl=1 "Hercules DJControl JogVision Top")](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/preview.jpg?fit=1030%2C1030&ssl=1)

Hercules has always offered some of the best quality audio interfaces in the business, which shouldn’t be a surprise as stand-alone audio interfaces was their primary business early on. Quality audio drivers and support for OS updates are always readily available. The **DJControl JogVision** combines Hercules’ quality audio interface support with a modern, advanced control surface complete with display read-outs at the center of each jog wheel.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Hercules DJ Control JogVision Front and Back](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/HERCULES-DJ-CONTROL-JOG-VISION-2.jpg?fit=300%2C300&ssl=1 "Hercules DJ Control JogVision Front and Back")](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/HERCULES-DJ-CONTROL-JOG-VISION-2.jpg?fit=600%2C600&ssl=1)

Right away, when you fire the **DJControl JogVision** up you’ll be struck by it’s feature packed, albeit a little tight, control layout — and the jog wheel digital displays are a really nice touch. This unit easy separates itself from some of the workhorse Hercules DJ controllers of the past (such as the RMX and RMX2) – it’s a pro-level DJ controller [DEX 3 DJ mixing software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) users should take into strong consideration.

All features found on the Hercules **DJControl JogVision** are DEX 3 supported, including the jog wheel displays, the beats ‘counter’, the effects controls, hot cue/loop pads and browser functions. While a challenge initially for our controller mapping team, particularly the display’s, it was worth the effort. This is a great option for DEX 3 DJs whether mixing music or music videos.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Hercules DJControl JogVision Angled](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/hercules-djcontrol-jogvision-top-right-angle_960x540-1.jpg?fit=300%2C169&ssl=1 "Hercules DJControl JogVision Angled")](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/hercules-djcontrol-jogvision-top-right-angle%5F960x540-1.jpg?fit=960%2C540&ssl=1)

The map for the **DJControl JogVision** is already included with [DEX 3.7.0.1](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) (and up) – just install the drivers for the JogVision and launch [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and you’re ready to mix.

#### **Here’s how Hercules DJ describes the DJControl JogVision:**

**DJControl Jogvision** is the first mobile Hercules DJ controller to feature a display in the center of each jog wheel, simultaneously indicating both the playback speed and position in the track at a glance. These displays allow DJs to spend less time looking at a computer screen, for enhanced focus, faster responsiveness and better track syncing – paying more attention to the music and their audience instead.
